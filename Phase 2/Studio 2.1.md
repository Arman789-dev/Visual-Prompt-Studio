Prompt A:
Design me a watercolor painting of a scene in which there is a snow-capped mountain landscape with a glowing radiant sun in the back. Flowing river, rugged and rocky rocks and short but sharp straight tress creating a contrast with the mountains. This scene is calm and serene creating a joyful mood. Use watercolor paints and brushes to design this scene. Overall, the features should be having an emphasis on lines, color, shape, texture, and the design should be evenly distributed. 


Prompt B:
Design me a traditional relief printmaking which also shows the same snow-capped mountain landscape with a glowing radiant sun in the back. Flowing river, rugged and rocky rocks and short but sharp straight tress creating a contrast with the mountains. This scene is calm and serene creating a joyful mood but in dark. Use a printing block, some carving tools and black printing ink. Overall, the features should be having an emphasis on carved lines, color, organic shapes, rough texture, and the design should be evenly distributed
